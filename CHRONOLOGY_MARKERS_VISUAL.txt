┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    COORDINATE MODE EXAMPLES                             │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

    1. RADAR VIEW (CIRCULAR)
       - Markers appear at cartesian X,Y positions
       - Circular range rings visible
       - Azimuth lines radiate from center
       - Most intuitive for radar data

    2. CARTESIAN (X, Y)
       - Markers at raw X,Y coordinates
       - Grid background
       - Standard graph axes
       - Good for precise measurements

    3. POLAR (RANGE, AZIMUTH)
       - X-axis: Azimuth (degrees)
       - Y-axis: Range (km)
       - Markers transformed to polar
       - Useful for range-bearing analysis
